I finally finished my first custom keyboard build, and I am ridiculously proud of how it turned out. This is a 75 percent layout with a fully custom PCB, case, and firmware setup. It is my personal daily driver now, and it feels great to use something that I designed end to end.

<img src="{{ "/assets/img/keyboard_setup.png" | relative_url }}" alt="Full desk shot of the keyboard with lighting" style="max-width: 720px; width: 100%; height: auto; display: block; margin: 0 auto;">

## What I built

This project, OP Keyboard, is a complete custom mechanical keyboard with a focus on usability and tweakability:

- 75 percent layout that keeps function keys but stays compact
- Custom PCB and case, both designed specifically for this board
- Powered by a Raspberry Pi Pico
- Two PCB build: a daughterboard (switchboard) and a main board that houses the dev board
- Sandwich mount with a pin-connector socket between boards for reverse compatibility
- QMK firmware for deep customization
- VIA support for quick, on-the-fly remapping
- A rotary encoder for media control or any macro you want
- Open hardware files so it can be modified or remixed

## Firmware and configurability

The firmware is built on QMK, which means it is as flexible as it gets. I can switch layers, remap keys, and change encoder behavior without rebuilding the whole setup. VIA support makes it even easier to experiment and dial in the perfect layout.

## Current issues and fixes in progress

V1 is solid, but there are a couple things I want to improve:

- The case is taller than I would like
- Main board v1.0 does not support the rotary encoder because of a design mistake; I am working on a v1.1 to fix this
