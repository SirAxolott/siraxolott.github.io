This update took almost a year, and I wanted to explain why.

Over the summer, I did not think about the keyboard project much. When I came back to university in the fall, I started designing V2 with per-key LEDs. But I ended up making so many design changes that it took the entire fall semester and part of the spring semester just to get a PCB design that I believe might work. With coursework at the same time, I was not able to consistently work on the project over the past year.

## What I am working on right now

### 1) V1.1 case revamp and mounting changes

I am currently working on a revamped version of the V1 case for V1.1. A major focus is the mounting method: this revision highlights a gasket-mounting approach instead of the tray-mount method I used in V1.

### 2) Knob circuit issue

As mentioned in the earlier project post, the knob circuit does not work right now because of a PCB design mistake. I am still iterating on this so the encoder support works properly in the updated revision.

### 3) Top cover redesign

I also updated the top-cover design with a more grid-like feature to better encase the switch-section zones.

More updates soon as I validate these hardware changes.
